How to read this. REPORTED by Omolewa: a rule that waits until your last three rewarded ads were finished, a loaded ad is worth at least 3.5× your last, and you are past your record by a set step, then asks for a “lethal” hand. Another rule rolls 20% per deal once you are 10% past your record. Others ask for hard pieces when you play fast near your record, or easy pieces after losses.
