Chapter 6 · 20:20 · What does a delivery earn?

The number on the screen, and the hours behind it

ILLUSTRATIVE MODELA toy calculation. Values are not averages and do not represent any platform.

Formula: remaining = payout − (distance × cost per km). Hourly = remaining ÷ (ride + wait + between orders) × 60. Excludes incentives, tips, fuel price changes, maintenance beyond the per-km input, phone data, taxes and breaks.
